Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 16, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Swollotto, the habit tracking RPG. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Swollotto:

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication information.
  • Habit data: Information about the habits you create, track, and complete.
  • Game progress: Experience points, stats, streaks, and other gameplay elements.
  • Display name: Your chosen display name for the leaderboard (You may choose to stay anonymous).
  • Communication preferences: Whether you've opted to receive news and updates.
  • Usage data: Basic analytics about how you interact with our app to improve user experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our habit tracking and RPG services
  • Authenticate you and secure your account
  • Track your habits and progress within the game
  • Display your chosen name, or anonymous, and progress on leaderboards
  • Send news and updates (only if you opt in)
  • Improve and optimize the app performance and user experience

We will never sell or provide your personal information to any third parties.

Leaderboards and Social Features

Swollotto includes leaderboard features that display user progress. Please note:

  • Participation in leaderboards:
  • You can choose to be completely anonymous
  • You can select any display name you prefer

Communications

We respect your communication preferences:

  • You can choose whether to receive news and updates during account creation
  • You can update your communication preferences at any time within the app
  • We will never contact users who have opted out of communications

Data Storage and Security

We use Google Firebase to store your data and manage authentication. Your data is stored securely in Firebase's cloud infrastructure with industry-standard security measures in place.

Firebase collects and processes certain data as outlined in their privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Your Rights and Control Over Your Data

You have the following rights and controls:

  • Delete any of your habit data directly within the app
  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of your personal information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt in or out of communications
  • Opt in or out of leaderboards
  • Change your display name at any time

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
