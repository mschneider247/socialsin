HabitBeast Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026  |  Effective Date: February 24, 2026

1. Introduction

HabitBeast ("the App") is operated by Social Sin LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use HabitBeast.

By using HabitBeast, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2a. Information You Provide

  • Email address — used to create and sign in to your account, whether you sign up with email, Google, or Apple. Guests can play without one until they save their account.
  • Monster name — the name you choose for your in-game monster (not required to be a real name)
  • Habit data — habits you select and track (habit types, completion status, streak counts, tier progression)
  • Battle data — battle actions earned, challenge history, win/loss records
  • Reading data — titles and authors of books you log, finish, or rate
  • Lab TODO tasks — tasks you type or dictate on the Lab TODO board, stored privately so only you can see them
  • Body profile (optional, only if you set up meal tracking) — age, sex, height, current and goal weight, activity level, and weight goal. Used only to calculate your calorie and macro targets, and never shown to other players.
  • Food, water, and weight logs (optional) — the meals and foods you log, water intake, weigh-ins, and personal foods you create, stored privately so only you can see them. Foods you submit to the shared food library are shared with other players.

2b. Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data — app screens visited, features used, session duration (via Firebase Analytics)
  • Crash reports — error logs and stack traces when the app crashes (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Device information — device type, operating system version, app version (for crash diagnostics only)
  • Push notification token — a device token from Firebase Cloud Messaging, used only to send you game notifications

2c. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Real name, physical address, or phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Photos or camera data
  • Audio recordings (voice features turn your speech into text on the spot — see 2d)
  • Financial information (payment and billing are handled entirely by Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and RevenueCat — we never see your card details)

2d. Microphone and Speech

HabitBeast uses your microphone only for two optional voice features, and only while you have the microphone switched on:

  • Lab TODO dictation — tap 🎙 on the Lab TODO board and speak your tasks.
  • Timer voice commands — turn on 🎙 VOICE on a habit timer and say START, STOP, or LOG.

Your speech is turned into text by your device's built-in speech recognition (Google's speech services on Android, Apple's on iOS). Depending on your device and settings, that service may process the audio on your device or send it to Google or Apple under their privacy policies. HabitBeast never records, stores, or uploads audio, and we never receive it — only the resulting text.

  • Timer voice commands are matched on your device and then discarded. Nothing is saved.
  • Dictated Lab TODO text appears in the task box for you to review. It is saved only if you tap ADD, exactly like a typed task, in your private task list that only you can access.

You can turn either feature off at any time, or turn off microphone access in your device settings. Everything else in HabitBeast works without it.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud) in the us-central1 region
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (Firebase default encryption)
  • Access to your data is controlled by Firebase Security Rules — your habits, habit logs, Lab TODO tasks, and food, water, and weight logs are readable only by you, a battle is visible only to the two players in it, and your public profile is visible to other signed-in players (see section 7)

6. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

HabitBeast offers optional paid subscriptions ("Beast Mode") and a one-time lifetime purchase ("Founding Beast") through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

  • What we collect: We receive a subscription status (active, expired, or lifetime) from RevenueCat after your purchase is verified by Apple or Google. We do not receive your payment method, card details, or billing address.
  • Subscription management: You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your device's App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • Refunds: Refund requests are handled by Apple or Google according to their respective policies — we cannot issue refunds directly.
  • Existing users grandfathered: Users who joined before May 2026 have been granted lifetime founder access at no charge.

7. Leaderboards and Your Public Profile

Other signed-in players can see your monster's public profile: its name, level, appearance, and game stats, plus the books on your public bookshelf. It appears in places like battles, rivals, friends, and leaderboards. Your email address is never shown to other players, though a player who already knows it can use it to find you and send a friend request.

If you choose to appear on the leaderboard (opt-in setting), your monster is also listed in leaderboard rankings. You can opt out of the leaderboard at any time in your Profile settings.

8. Children's Privacy

HabitBeast is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated data
  • Correction — update inaccurate information in your profile
  • Opt-out — disable analytics tracking or leaderboard visibility

Deleting Your HabitBeast Account

You can delete your HabitBeast account and its data yourself, in the app, at any time. This works for guest accounts too.

In the app (immediate):

  1. Open HabitBeast and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap delete account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

In older versions of the app, delete account opens an email to us instead. That works too.

Without the app: email socialsinllc@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account" and include the email address on your account. We delete the account within 30 days of your request. Guest accounts have no email address, so delete those in the app. An unused guest account is also deleted automatically after 30 days (see Data Retention).

What is deleted:

  • Your login and email address
  • Your monster: its name, appearance, level, stats, closet, and brain slugs
  • Your habits, habit history, streaks, and habit stacks
  • Your food, water, and weight logs, your body profile, and your personal foods
  • Your Lab TODO tasks and Dig progress
  • Your bookshelf and book ratings (your ratings come off each book's score)
  • Every battle you fought and its replay, for both players, plus any pending challenges
  • Your friendships, friend requests, and friend group memberships
  • Player reports about your monster, and your account ID on any reports you filed
  • Your monster's portrait image

Your monster leaves the leaderboards within an hour.

What is kept:

  • Approved food-library entries you submitted stay in the shared food library, because other players log them. They are no longer linked to you. Pending and rejected submissions are deleted.
  • Usage analytics and crash reports that were already collected (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics). They are tied to a random account ID, never to your name or email address, and once your account is deleted nothing connects that ID to you.
  • Purchase records held by Google Play, the App Store, and RevenueCat. They keep these under their own policies for billing, refunds, and tax.
  • Other players' own stats, such as XP and wins from battles against your monster, stay on their accounts.

Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a Beast Mode subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store first, or it will keep billing.

10. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained while your account is active
  • Deleted accounts: Deleted immediately when you delete in the app, or within 30 days of an email request. See Deleting Your HabitBeast Account for the little that is kept.
  • Guest accounts: A guest account that hasn't opened the app for 30 days is deleted automatically, along with all of its data
  • Usage analytics and crash reports: Tied to a random account ID, not to your name or email address. They may be retained after an account is deleted, for aggregate statistics.
